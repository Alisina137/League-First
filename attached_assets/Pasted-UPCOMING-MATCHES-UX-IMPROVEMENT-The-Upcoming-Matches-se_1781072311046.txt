UPCOMING MATCHES UX IMPROVEMENT

The Upcoming Matches section should intelligently handle cases where there are no upcoming matches in the near future.

GOAL

Never show an empty Upcoming Matches area.

Always provide useful information to the user.

---

## STATE 1: UPCOMING MATCHES EXIST

If there are matches scheduled within the next 10 days:

Display normal match cards.

Example:

Real Madrid vs Barcelona
Jun 15, 2026
20:00

Manchester City vs Arsenal
Jun 17, 2026
18:00

---

## STATE 2: NO MATCHES WITHIN 10 DAYS

If there are NO matches scheduled in the next 10 days:

Replace match cards with:

---

COMING SOON
No matches are scheduled within the next 10 days.
-------------------------------------------------

Also display:

Next Match Scheduled:
[Date]

Example:

COMING SOON

No matches are scheduled within the next 10 days.

Next Match Scheduled:
July 12, 2026

---

## STATE 3: TOURNAMENT NOT STARTED YET

For tournaments such as:

* FIFA World Cup
* Champions League
* Europa League

If the tournament has not started:

Show:

---

## TOURNAMENT STARTS SOON

Competition:
FIFA World Cup

Tournament Begins:
June 11, 2026

Days Remaining:
18

---

Include a countdown badge:

18 Days Remaining

---

## API LOGIC

Fetch:

Upcoming Matches

If result count > 0:
Show match cards.

Else:

Fetch next available fixture date.

If date exists:

Show:

Coming Soon

Next Match: <Date>

If no fixtures exist:

Show:

Schedule not yet announced.

---
